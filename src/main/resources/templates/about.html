About The Initiative

Tamil Nadu Smart Hospital Portal

A digital health scheduling architecture and demonstration platform

Purpose & Vision

The Tamil Nadu Smart Hospital platform is an advanced web-based healthcare access prototype built to connect patients across all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu with multi-specialty hospitals, clinical practitioners, and outpatient time slots.

Architectural Highlights

  • Robust Java Backend: Engineered on modern Java with Spring Boot 3.3 and Spring MVC.
  • Accessible Server-Side Views: Dynamic server-rendered HTML using Thymeleaf templates with semantic markup and visible focus outlines.
  • Thread-Safe Concurrency Protection: Concurrency check and database-level transactional locks to prevent double-booking of any time slot.
  • Role-Aware Access: Segregated roles and dashboards for Patients, Doctors, and Administrators with simulated demo sign-in shortcuts.
  • Local In-Memory Persistence: Powered by an embedded H2 database with automatic schema generation and fictional demo seed data.

Demonstration Scope & Privacy Standards

⚠️ Demonstration Safety Notice:

All hospital names, doctor qualifications, time slots, and appointments are synthetic demo records. No real medical records, government ID numbers (Aadhaar), or payments are stored or requested. Real appointments must be confirmed directly with official healthcare providers.
