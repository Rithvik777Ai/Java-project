Platform Notice & Patient Safety

This application is a demonstration prototype developed for the Tamil Nadu healthcare technology evaluation. All hospital listings, doctor names, OPD schedules, ICU metrics, and appointment availability are purely sample fictional records. No real health records, payment details, or government identity numbers are collected.
